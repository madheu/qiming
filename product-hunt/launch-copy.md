**Maker comment / description**

Hànzi helps you find a Chinese name that feels natural—not just a word-for-word translation.

Enter your name, choose an energy, and get three considered options with:

• Chinese characters
• Mandarin pinyin with tone marks
• Character-by-character meanings
• A short explanation of why the name works
• Copy and share buttons

There’s no sign-up and no naming API. It runs in your browser, so you can explore without handing your name to a naming service.

We also added a small Chinese zodiac guide: find your animal from your birth year, confirm January/February birthdays with the Lunar New Year boundary, and explore the 2026 Year of the Horse through cultural notes and modern reflection prompts—not fixed predictions.

Built for Chinese learners, travelers, writers, students, and anyone curious about carrying a little more meaning into a name.

Try it here: https://chinesename.cc.cd/

## Maker first comment

Hi Product Hunt 👋

I built Hànzi because most Chinese name generators stop at a transliteration—or give you a random string of characters with no context.

A Chinese name should be something you can understand, pronounce, and feel comfortable carrying. Hànzi gives you three starting points based on your name and the energy you choose, then explains the characters and the feeling behind each one.

A few things I cared about:

• natural names over literal translations
• tone-marked pinyin, not just romanized syllables
• meanings explained in plain English
• no account and no server-side naming API
• a calm, editorial experience instead of a noisy randomizer

I’ve also started a Chinese zodiac guide for people who want to explore the culture around their name. It handles the Lunar New Year boundary for January and February birthdays, and keeps cultural symbolism separate from prediction.

I’d love to know: did you choose a Chinese name before, and what made it feel right—or wrong?
