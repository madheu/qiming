06 / A few names to get you curious

Chinese name examples with meanings

These examples combine surnames and given names from our curated collection. In Chinese, the surname comes first. The explanations below are character glosses and poetic interpretations, not literal translations of English names.

林知远

Lín Zhīyuǎn

林 = forest (surname) · 知 = to know · 远 = far-reaching.

A thoughtful name suggesting wisdom that reaches beyond the obvious.

周予安

Zhōu Yǔ’ān

周 = Zhou (surname) · 予 = to give · 安 = peace.

A gentle interpretation: peace, freely given.

沈明澈

Shěn Míngchè

沈 = Shen (surname) · 明 = bright · 澈 = clear.

A bright, clear-hearted feeling with a calm rhythm.

许嘉言

Xǔ Jiāyán

许 = Xu (surname) · 嘉 = excellent · 言 = word.

A name inspired by thoughtful words and kindness.

07 / A little cultural context

How to choose a Chinese name

Start with meaning

Choose qualities you would enjoy carrying: calm, warmth, curiosity or courage. A Chinese given name often consists of one or two meaningful characters.

Keep the name together

The surname normally comes first, followed by the given name. In Lín Zhīyuǎn, Lín is the surname and Zhīyuǎn is one given name, not a first name and a middle name.

Try it out loud

Pinyin shows Mandarin pronunciation, including tone marks. Ask a fluent speaker to help you say the name and check its associations before using it in an important or lasting context.

08 / Good questions

Chinese name generator FAQ

Is this Chinese name generator free?

Yes. Generate three names at a time, explore more options, and copy or share your favorite. No account or payment is needed.

Can I get a Chinese name from my English name?

Yes. Your name helps seed the selection, and your chosen style helps shape the options. This is a curated name generator, not a certified translation or a custom consultation.

Do the names include pinyin and meanings?

Each result includes Chinese characters, tone-marked Mandarin pinyin, character meanings, style tags and an explanation of the name’s feeling.

Is this a translation of my English name?

No. An English name can be transliterated into Chinese to approximate its sound, but choosing a Chinese name is a different task. Hànzi offers name suggestions rather than a direct translation.

Do I need to sign up, and where does my name go?

No sign-up is required. Name generation runs in your browser; the entered English name is not sent to a naming service. The site uses analytics for visits and interactions.

Can I use the name for my Chinese class?

Yes, it can be a starting point. Ask your teacher or a fluent speaker to check pronunciation and suitability. For legal documents or permanent uses, seek qualified guidance.
