A Hànzi culture guide · 字 zì

What is a Chinese
courtesy name?

A courtesy name, written 字 and pronounced zì, is a traditional name used in addition to a person’s given name. It was associated with adulthood, education, friendship, and public or literary life.

What does 字 mean?

In historical Chinese naming culture, a person’s 名 was the given name, while the 字 offered another way to address that person. Friends and peers could use the courtesy name as a sign of respect and familiarity. It was not a translation of the given name and it was not a legal name.

Modern context: most Chinese speakers do not use courtesy names in everyday life. Today, a 字 is best understood as a cultural, historical, literary, or creative naming practice.

名, 字, and 号 are different.

TermEnglishPurpose
名Given nameEveryday personal name and official identity.
字Courtesy nameTraditional peer-facing name, often related to the 名.
号Art or studio nameSelf-chosen literary, artistic, or philosophical identity.

How was a 字 related to a 名?

Echo

诸葛亮 was 字孔明. The characters 亮 and 明 both belong to the field of brightness, but the second is not simply a duplicate.

Contrast

朱熹 was 字元晦. 熹 suggests light; 晦 gives that light a quieter, inward counterpart.

Extension

A name about knowing may lead to a 字 about distance, wisdom, or asking. The second name continues the first idea.
