A Chinese courtesy name is traditionally used among peers, friends, and literary circles. It grows from your given name through meaning—not from a random pile of attractive characters.

This tool creates a cultural suggestion, not a legal name. Modern Chinese speakers do not usually use courtesy names in daily life.

03 / The relationship matters

A 字 should explain the 名.

明 → 子晦

opposite · complementary contrast

明 carries brightness; 晦 holds its quieter inward half. The contrast gives the pair tension instead of repetition.

明 → 子昭

synonym · classical echo

昭 belongs to the same field of light, but feels more formal and literary. It answers the name without copying it.
