03 / A necessary distinction

Shared characters do not guarantee shared meaning.

Meaning is not copying

Some kanji look familiar but behave differently in Japanese and Chinese. A careful result checks the semantic field before reusing a character.

Sound is an approximation

Japanese katakana can help you approach a Mandarin name, but it does not represent the four tones or every consonant contrast.
